# Voice — how Clinton writes

Basis: Clinton's own CV (see `skills/custom/clinton-cv/references/source-cv.md`) and his corrections while reviewing the Rituals CV ("the way the points are written don't sound like me"). Every sentence in a CV or letter must pass the test: *would Clinton say this out loud in an interview?*

## Patterns to copy

- **First person, full sentences.** "I was responsible for the networking infrastructure at…", "I project managed network hardware deployments for approximately 150 stores worldwide.", "With my fellow team members I was responsible for 1000+ retail stores."
- **Plain verbs.** was responsible for, managed, built, wrote, advised on, worked with, implemented, replaced, reported to, did.
- **Concrete numbers and names.** 7 offices, about 1500 employees, 3 branches, approximately 150 stores, Cisco core, Radware WAF, Raytheon proxy, Asterisk, SolarWinds, PIX.
- **His phrases.** "3rd party vendors", "3rd line support", "day to day", "business cases", "stakeholders", "the fix goes into the standard, not just the ticket", "so the next person does not start from scratch", "my retail experience is not based on Rituals alone".
- **Context before claim.** He sets the scene (company size, who he reported to) and then says what he did.
- **Honest scope.** "I would call that working knowledge rather than Jamf administration, and I would say so in the interview."

## Words and shapes to avoid

- Noun-phrase fragments: "Owned network architecture for…", "Platform engineering on an AWS estate; investigate…"
- Résumé-speak: owned, leveraged, codified, spearheaded, drove, structural fix, supplier governance, results-driven, proven track record, cross-functional initiatives, operational excellence, muscle.
- Addressing the employer inside CV bullets ("relevant to Rituals' …"). That goes in the cover letter.
- Repeating a vendor or fact more than once per document.
- Negative disclaimers in the CV body ("not a Jamf specialist"). Scope limits go in the letter, phrased positively.

## XYZ in his voice

The XYZ formula (accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z) still works in first person. Keep the number only if it is in `facts.md`.

- "At the start of the 2020 lockdown I built an ISP redundant Cisco AnyConnect VPN in a very short time so everyone could work from home. It has had 100% uptime since March 2020."
- "I project managed Meraki hardware rollouts to approximately 150 stores worldwide, from design through handover to the support teams."

## Letter voice

Same person, slightly warmer. Short paragraphs, each making one point. Opens with the role and why he fits in two sentences; closes with "I would welcome the chance to talk about the role." and "Kind regards,". Dutch work-status sentence stays in NL applications.
