# Northwind Retail — Service Delivery Manager

Source: example posting (fictional) · Location: Amsterdam, 3 days on site · Contact: Alex Recruiter

## About the role

Northwind Retail runs 250 stores across the Benelux and a growing online channel. As Service Delivery Manager you own the day-to-day IT service to the stores and head office. You chair the weekly service review with our outsourced service desk and field-service partners, make sure they deliver to their SLAs, and lead problem management so that recurring incidents are solved structurally rather than one ticket at a time.

## What you will do

- Own incident, problem and change management for store and head-office IT (ITIL)
- Chair weekly and monthly service reviews with two outsourced providers; hold them to their SLAs
- Lead root cause analysis on recurring store incidents (POS, payment terminals, network) with suppliers
- Build and maintain service reporting (we use ServiceNow and Power BI)
- Keep runbooks and knowledge articles current in Confluence
- Drive continuous improvement: reduce repeat incidents, improve first-contact resolution

## What we ask

- 5+ years in IT service management or service operations, ideally in retail
- Experience managing outsourced suppliers and SLAs
- ITIL 4 Foundation; ServiceNow experience
- Strong reporting skills; Power BI is a plus
- Experience using AI tools to speed up analysis or reporting is a plus
- Fluent English; Dutch is an advantage
