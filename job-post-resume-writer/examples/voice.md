# Voice — Sam Example

Built from three samples: a LinkedIn post about the portal project, an internal end-of-quarter email, and the profile text of the old CV. Every CV line must pass the test: *would Sam say this out loud in an interview?*

## Patterns to copy

- First person, short sentences, one point each: "I own the weekly service review.", "I built the reporting in Power BI."
- Plain verbs: own, built, lead, ran, wrote, chased, trained, introduced, fixed.
- Names the tool and the number in the same sentence: "moved ticket intake from email to the ServiceNow portal, which cut duplicate tickets by 40%".
- Explains the why in everyday terms: "fixing the process rather than the symptom", "numbers they trust".
- Mild, direct humour about chasing suppliers; never sarcastic in writing.

## Their phrases

- "the same problem keeps coming back"
- "fix the process rather than the symptom"
- "numbers they trust"
- "chased them on delivery dates"
- "keep them current"

## Words and shapes to avoid

- Noun fragments: "Ownership of weekly service review cadence".
- Résumé-speak: spearheaded, leveraged, drove, synergy, stakeholder alignment, best-in-class, results-driven, proven track record, passionate.
- Passive voice: "reporting was implemented".
- Claims about teams Sam did not lead or numbers Sam cannot show.

## Rewrite examples

| Not Sam's voice | Sam's voice |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Spearheaded the migration of ticket intake to a self-service portal, driving a 40% reduction in duplicate tickets. | I ran the project that moved ticket intake from email to the ServiceNow portal, which cut duplicate tickets by 40% in the first quarter. |
| Responsible for stakeholder alignment across outsourced vendors. | I lead the monthly problem review with our two outsourced providers and track their SLA performance. |
| Leveraged AI to optimise reporting workflows. | I use Copilot in Power BI and a ChatGPT workflow to draft the weekly review commentary from the data, which took prep from about 3 hours to under 1 hour. |

## XYZ in their voice

- "I cut duplicate tickets by 40% in the first quarter after go-live by moving ticket intake from email to the ServiceNow portal."
- "I took first-contact resolution from 55% to 72% in a year by introducing a daily stand-up and a shared knowledge base."

## Letter voice

Same person, a little warmer. Four or five short paragraphs. Opens with the role and the two things that fit best; closes with "I would be glad to talk it through." and "Kind regards,".
